Upload & Rules

Drop in this cycle's raw files. Smartverse reshapes and joins them itself — Emp Code, Login ID and Name are used to match records across files — then applies the rules below automatically.
📥 Source files
Daily Performance Report is required (it's the attendance spine). Users, Employee Master and the Activity log are optional — each adds one more layer: designation & active status, reporting head, and GPS/State verification respectively.
⚙️ Attendance Rules — by designation
Full Day / Half Day / Absent are hour thresholds; Early Going is flagged as a note, not a separate status. Ranges accept plain text like "4.5 to 8" or "*" for none.
Designation Timing Method Full Day ≥ (hrs) Half Day range Early Going range Absent below (hrs) Note
🧭 Beat Coverage & Market Working Rules
Ensures beat compliance and minimum field time. Refines a "Present" day into P / P1 / P2 / P3 — P1 = worked outside the beat radius, P2 = minimum market hours not met, P3 = no manual logout recorded.
Designation Min. market working hours
🔍 Verification sensitivity
Controls when a "Present" day gets flagged for manual verification against the GPS log.
👤 Employee visibility
Uses each employee's current status from the Users file. If they're marked Inactive but still had at least one real worked day this period, they stay in the report with days after their last active day shown as INACTIVE instead of Absent. If they're Inactive with zero real worked days anywhere this period, they're left out entirely. A day with no record at all (before joining) is shown as NA.
